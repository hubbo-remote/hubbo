### Anything unexpected
- **Loop gaps while idle.** `loop: max gap` reached 30634 µs, and `iterations over 5 ms` climbed steadily: 7 at 30 s, 52 at ~3 min (during pairing), 561 at 1140 s, 680 at 1380 s, 789 at 1620 s, 823 at 1813 s. That's about 0.45 per second with no radio or HTTP traffic and BLE connected but idle. Worth checking before steps 9 and 25, which expect `loop: max gap` to stay small.
- The board got 192.168.1.196, the old Harmonizer address mentioned in the handoff. The handoff says the old OTA settings don't apply, but if anything on the LAN still targets .196 as "Harmonizer", it now reaches Hubbo.
- The first boot's top lines were not captured (see Boot log). Indirect evidence that the format happened: the first boot's `status` showed `at boot config: fresh, generation 1`, and later boots mount cleanly.
